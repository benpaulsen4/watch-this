# BrandFoundations

Reference card for shape, spacing and elevation.

**Don't compose with this component.** Radii: `rounded-lg` for buttons, inputs
and cards; `rounded-xl` for Card and Modal; `rounded-full` for badges, avatars
and switch tracks. Spacing runs on the 2/3/4/6/8 steps. Elevation is a tinted
shadow (`shadow-xl shadow-black/25`), never a lighter surface — everything sits
on a dark ground.
