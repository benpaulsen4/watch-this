If you can't find a Series Finale you were expecting, it's usually one of the reasons below.

## The Year Isn't Over Yet Where You Are

A Series Finale becomes available once 31 December has ended **in your profile's timezone**. If your timezone is set to somewhere behind you, your recap will arrive later than you expect.

You can check it on the **Profile** tab of your profile page. Read: [Edit Your Profile: Name, Photo, and Timezone](/help/profile/edit-your-profile).

If you open a year that isn't available, you'll see this:

![The message shown when a year has no Series Finale](/help/series-finale/no-series-finale.webp)

## You Hadn't Started Using WatchThis Yet

Series Finale covers each year from when you started using WatchThis. A year with no activity, or one before you joined, has nothing to recap.

This includes history you **imported** from another app. Imported episodes are often dated by when they aired rather than when you watched them, so years before you joined WatchThis don't get a Series Finale, even if your import has entries dated then.

## You Dismissed the Banner

Selecting **Not now** hides the dashboard banner, but the Series Finale is still there. Find it under **Profile → Data Management → Series Finale**. Read: [Find Past Years](/help/series-finale/find-past-years).

## It's an Older Year That Hasn't Appeared Yet

If you've used WatchThis for several years, older years are added a few at a time. Your newest year is always ready first. Visit your dashboard or profile again and the rest will fill in.

## It Was a Quiet Year

If you watched fewer than 10 episodes **and** finished fewer than 5 titles, the year is too quiet for a full Series Finale. It's still listed in your profile, but opening it shows a short note instead of the story and recap, and there's no share card or dashboard banner for it.

To get a full Series Finale next year, keep marking episodes watched and setting titles to **Completed** as you go. Read: [Watch Status Explained](/help/tracking/watch-status-explained).
