Every Series Finale you've had is kept in your profile, so you can go back to any year whenever you like. Recaps don't expire.

## Open a Past Year

1. Go to your **Profile**.
2. Open the **Data Management** tab.
3. In the **Series Finale** section, select **Open** next to the year you want.

![The Series Finale archive in your profile, listing 2025, 2024 and 2023](/help/series-finale/profile-archive.webp)

Years are listed newest first, with each year's episode and title counts. The newest is highlighted.

The archive is also where to find a Series Finale after selecting **Not now** on the dashboard banner. The banner only promotes your newest year, and only until you dismiss it.

## Which Years Are Listed

You'll find a Series Finale for every completed calendar year from when you started using WatchThis. A year shows up once it has ended in your profile's timezone.

If you've used WatchThis for several years, the archive may fill in over a few visits. The newest year is always ready first, and older years are added each time you come back to your dashboard or profile.

## Quiet Years

Years where you watched fewer than 10 episodes and finished fewer than 5 titles are still listed. Opening one shows a short note instead of the full recap:

![The note shown for a quiet year](/help/series-finale/thin-year.webp)

## Past Years Don't Change

Each Series Finale is a snapshot taken when it was first put together. Updating your history afterwards (marking an old film completed, say, or un-ticking episodes) won't change a year's recap. That keeps your recap matching any card you've already shared.

The one exception is your friends. If someone in your crew later turns off crew comparisons, they're removed from your past recaps too, and anyone who changes their username shows under their new name.
