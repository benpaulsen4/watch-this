The story is your Series Finale told one card at a time: your hours, your top show, your most obscure film, your genres, your busiest month, your biggest day, your watching type, the shows you walked out on, your crew, and how you compare with a friend.

![Four cards from a Series Finale story: the opening card, hours watched, top show, and biggest day](/help/series-finale/story-cards.webp)

## Open the Story

- **On a phone:** select **See your Series Finale** on the dashboard banner, or **Open** next to the year in your profile. The first time you open a year on your phone, it always starts with the story.
- **On a computer:** open your Series Finale, then select **Play as story** at the top of the page.

## Move Through the Cards

- **Next card:** tap the right side of the screen, or press the **→** key.
- **Previous card:** tap the left side of the screen, or press the **←** key.
- **Close:** tap **×** in the top corner, or press **Esc**.

The bars along the top show where you are in the story. If a card is taller than your screen, scroll down to see the rest of it; each new card starts back at the top.

Cards with nothing to say are skipped. For example, if you didn't finish any films that year, there's no "most obscure film" card, and if you don't share any lists, there are no crew cards.

## The Last Card

The story ends on a summary of your year: your hours, episodes, titles finished, shows abandoned, top show, deepest cut and watching type. From here you can:

- select **Share your card** to share your year as an image (read [Share Your Card](/help/series-finale/share-your-card));
- select **See the full recap** to open the full page.

![The summary card at the end of the story, with Share your card and See the full recap](/help/series-finale/story-summary.webp)

## After You've Finished

Reaching the last card unlocks the full recap on your phone. WatchThis remembers this on your account, so it applies on every device you sign in on. From then on, opening that year on your phone goes straight to the full recap, and you can replay the story at any time with the **▷** (Play as story) button at the top.

If you close the story on your phone before reaching the end, you're taken back to your dashboard. Open it again whenever you're ready.

## Tips

- **Some cards depend on how you tick episodes.** The biggest-day timeline and anything about the time of day you watch need episodes you marked one at a time. Marking a whole season watched at once gives every episode the same timestamp, so those episodes can't say when you watched. With fewer than 50 episodes ticked one at a time in the year, the biggest-day card shows the date and episode count without the times.
- **Times are shown in your profile's timezone,** not the timezone of wherever you happen to be viewing from.
