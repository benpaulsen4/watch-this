Series Finale is your year on WatchThis, put back together from the episodes you ticked off and the titles you finished. It covers:

- how many hours you spent watching;
- your most-watched show and your most obscure film;
- the genres you leaned on and the month you watched the most;
- your biggest single day;
- the shows you gave up on;
- how you compare with the people you share lists with.

You can go through it three ways:

- **As a story.** One card at a time, made for your phone. Read [Watch Your Story](/help/series-finale/watch-your-story).
- **As a full recap.** Everything on one page, made for a bigger screen. Read [Explore Your Full Recap](/help/series-finale/explore-your-recap).
- **As a card to share.** A single image you can post or send. Read [Share Your Card](/help/series-finale/share-your-card).

## When It Arrives

Each Series Finale covers one calendar year, 1 January to 31 December. It becomes available as soon as the year is over **where you are**, based on the timezone in your profile. Someone in Sydney gets theirs before someone in London does.

When it's ready, a banner appears at the top of your dashboard. Select **See your Series Finale** to open it.

![The Series Finale banner on the dashboard](/help/series-finale/dashboard-banner.webp)

The banner goes away by itself once you've been through your Series Finale, either to the last card of the story or to the bottom of the full recap. Not in the mood yet? Select **Not now** to hide it. Either way, your Series Finale doesn't go anywhere; you can open it from your profile at any time. Read [Find Past Years](/help/series-finale/find-past-years).

The first time you open a year, it can take a few seconds to put together.

## What Counts

Series Finale only knows what you've told WatchThis, so it's as accurate as your tracking.

- **Episodes** count in the year you marked them watched.
- **Shows and films you finished** count in the year you set them to **Completed**.
- **Shows you dropped** count in the year you set them to **Dropped**.
- **Hours** add up the runtimes of those episodes and films, using runtimes from TMDB. If TMDB has no runtime for something, it's left out of the hours, and your recap says how many items that affected.

For how each status works, read [Watch Status Explained](/help/tracking/watch-status-explained).

## Your Watching Type

Most years also get a **watching type**: a name for the kind of viewer you were that year, with a picture to back it up. There are several types, and we're keeping them a surprise. You'll find out yours when you get there.

## A Few Things to Know

- **It doesn't change once it's made.** Your Series Finale is a snapshot. If you go back and edit last year's history later, the recap keeps what it said the first time.
- **A quiet year gets a short note instead.** If you watched fewer than 10 episodes and finished fewer than 5 titles, there isn't enough to tell a story. You'll see a short note rather than the full set of cards.
- **Years before you joined aren't covered.** History you imported from another app can be dated before you joined WatchThis; those years don't get a Series Finale.
- **Friends only appear with their agreement.** People you share lists with can turn crew comparisons off. Read [Friends in Your Series Finale](/help/series-finale/friends-in-your-series-finale).
