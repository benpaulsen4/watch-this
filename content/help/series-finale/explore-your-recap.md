The full recap puts your entire Series Finale on a single page. It's what opens on a computer, and on a phone once you've been through the story.

## Open the Recap

- **On a computer:** select **See your Series Finale** on the dashboard banner, or **Open** next to the year in your profile.
- **On a phone:** go through the story to the last card and select **See the full recap**. After that, the recap opens directly. Read [Watch Your Story](/help/series-finale/watch-your-story).

At the top of the page:

- **Play as story** replays the story.
- **Share** creates your share card. Read [Share Your Card](/help/series-finale/share-your-card).
- **←** takes you back to your profile.

## What's on the Page

### Your Year at a Glance

The page opens with your total hours, a line summing up your year, and four numbers: episodes watched, titles completed, your longest run of consecutive days watching, and shows dropped.

![The top of the recap: total hours, a summary line, and four headline numbers](/help/series-finale/recap-top.webp)

You may also see:

- **"Top N% of everyone on WatchThis".** How your hours compare with everyone else's recaps for a comparable year. It's anonymous, and it only appears once enough people have a recap to compare against.
- **"Excludes N episodes or films with no runtime on TMDB".** Those items count towards your episodes and titles but not your hours, because TMDB doesn't know how long they are.

### Watched by Month and Your Type

A chart of how much you watched each month, with your peak month called out, and how your watching fell across the days of the week. Next to it is your **watching type** and the picture behind it.

### Most Watched, and Least Known

Your most-watched show of the year (how many episodes, how long, and when you last watched it) and your most obscure film: the least popular film you finished, according to TMDB. If friends had the same top show, you'll see their names here too.

![Your most-watched show, your most obscure film, and your genres](/help/series-finale/recap-titles.webp)

### Genres

Your top genres across everything you finished. Each percentage is that genre's share of all the genre tags on those titles, and most titles carry two or three tags.

### Biggest Day

The day you watched the most episodes. When there's enough data, you'll also see when you started and stopped, each episode on a timeline, and a list of what you watched and when.

### Walked Out On

The shows you dropped that year, with the last episode you watched before giving up. Underneath are films that have been sitting in **Planning** the longest, with how many days they've been waiting.

![Your biggest day, with a timeline of what you watched, and the shows you walked out on](/help/series-finale/recap-biggest-day.webp)

### The Crew and You & a Friend

If you share lists with other people, the last row compares your year with theirs. Read [Friends in Your Series Finale](/help/series-finale/friends-in-your-series-finale).

## On a Phone

On a phone, the recap stacks everything into a single column. The **Play as story** and **Share** buttons shrink to icons at the top.

![The full recap on a phone](/help/series-finale/recap-phone.webp)

Panels with nothing to show are left out. For example, a year with no dropped shows and no waiting films has no "Walked out on" panel.
